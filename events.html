Private events

Graduations, bridal showers, engagement parties, rehearsal dinners, birthdays, baby showers — we love being part of them. From garden gatherings to cosy candlelit dinners, let us host the celebration while you simply enjoy it.

We have taken the restaurant fully private for weddings, receptions and intimate parties. What’s possible depends on the night and the season, so the honest answer is always a conversation — tell us the date and roughly how many, and we’ll tell you what we can do.
